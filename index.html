Who we are

A clear path from challenge to progress.

Founded in 2002, i3Core is a technology services company based in Ashburn, Virginia. We work with organizations at different stages, helping them identify where technology can make a meaningful difference.

From early planning to implementation and ongoing support, our approach starts by understanding your needs. Then we bring together industry knowledge and technical expertise to create integrated solutions built for real-world use.

We measure our work by the outcomes our clients achieve, and we stay committed to quality, responsiveness, and professional excellence at every step.
